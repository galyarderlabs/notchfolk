The island's six interaction sounds (open, close, tab, click, send, answer) are
synthesised by Notchfolk itself - sine bursts, sweeps and chimes written as PCM
in a one-off Python script during development (docs/island-sounds-plan.md). They
contain no third-party material and are dedicated to the public domain under
CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). You may use,
modify and redistribute them for any purpose, with no attribution required.
