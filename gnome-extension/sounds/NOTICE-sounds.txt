The island's eight interaction sounds are original syntheses by Notchfolk. The
six gesture sounds were generated by a one-off Python script; `drink.wav` and
`bite.wav` were synthesized as PCM from FFmpeg's built-in audio sources and
filters. None contain third-party material. All are dedicated to the public
domain under CC0 1.0 (https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
You may use, modify and redistribute them for any purpose, with no attribution required.
