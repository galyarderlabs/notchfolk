Everything you glance at, in one place.

Hover the notch to open it. Move away and it closes. Nothing to arrange, nothing to pin.

Music

See what's playing, skip, pause and seek — from Spotify, your browser, or any MPRIS player — without switching windows.

Synced lyrics

The island widens and takes the cover's colour, each line lighting up as it's sung. Click a line to jump there. Lyrics from LRCLIB.

Tasks & reminders

Jot a task, give it a time, and the notch taps you when it's due. Missed ones stay in the last 7 days, so nothing is lost.

Calendar

Today's events at a glance, with a gentle nudge before each one starts.

AI assistant

Ask in plain words and get an answer in the island. Claude, Codex, Hermes, OpenAI, and more — the provider you chose in Settings.

Voice control

Press Super+I and talk. Local Whisper keeps it offline, or point it at your own OpenAI-compatible router — the key stays in your desktop's secret store.

MCP server

56 tools your AI can use — tasks, calendar, reminders, files, and more — over a local server on your computer.

Focus timer

A calm Pomodoro with the whole round at a glance, a breathing Live Activity in the notch and gentle reminders.

Water & calories

Set your bottle size and daily goal, log meals, and see today's progress in the notch.

File shelf

Drag files onto the notch to keep them close, then drag them out into a chat, an email or another folder.

Prayer times

An optional page with today's prayers on a sun arc, gentle reminders, and music that can pause by itself. Calculated on your PC.

Clipboard history

What you copy waits beside your Shelf. Click to copy it again, or drag it straight into a chat.

A small companion that knows when to rest.

When nothing is happening, the clock fades and a pair of eyes appears. They doze off after a while, and wave hello when you come back.

Light on your PC

Notchfolk draws only while something moves. When it's idle it uses no CPU at all, even with music in the background.

Your data stays here

Tasks, water logs and shelf items are stored on your computer. There are no accounts and no tracking.

Made for GNOME

Built natively for GNOME 46–51, sharp on every display scale, and it steps aside for full-screen apps.

So light, you'll forget it's there.

Notchfolk is built natively for GNOME, not on a hidden web browser. It sits quietly until something happens, then gets out of the way again.

Make it yours.

Open Settings → Appearance. A few switches decide how Notchfolk behaves on your screen.

18:55

Settings → Appearance → Island

Black or Glass island

Notchfolk's own black island, or clear dark glass over what is behind it, blurred, with a lit rim. The slider sets how dark the glass is.

Notch or pill shape

Joined to the top of the screen, or a round pill floating just below it — the same island either way.

Standard or Large

The open island 600 or 720 px wide, its pages taller at Large, each laying itself out for the room.

Your clock, your format

24-hour or 12-hour, following your GNOME clock. The island's clock always matches.

Notch size

Adjust it yourself. It's automatically larger on wide monitors.

Follows GNOME

Accent colour and text size come from your GNOME settings — change them there and the island restyles itself.

Get Notchfolk for GNOME

GNOME 46–51 on Linux, x86_64.

Install with one command

Paste this in a terminal. It installs the extension and the daemon to your home folder, then log out and back in.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/galyarderlabs/notchfolk/main/get.sh | bash

Prefer a package? notchfolk-bin is prepared in packaging/aur, but AUR registration is paused, so it isn't published yet — yay won't find it. The command above is the way to install for now.

Why it's safe

  • Installs to your home folder. No admin rights, nothing system-wide.
  • Your settings stay as you set them. Run the same command again to update.
  • Remove it anytime with notchfolk-uninstall.
  • Your data stays on your computer. No accounts, no tracking. Synced lyrics come from LRCLIB.
  • Only install Notchfolk from this page or the official GitHub releases.

Questions

How do I install Notchfolk?

Paste the install command above in a terminal. It installs the extension and the daemon to your home folder, then log out and back in (or restart GNOME Shell on X11). Run the same command again to update; your settings stay as you set them.

Which GNOME versions work?

GNOME 46 to 51, on Linux (x86_64), Wayland or X11. It's a GNOME Shell extension, so it needs GNOME Shell.

How does voice control work?

Press Super+I and speak. Whisper runs on your device — set it up with notchfolk-setup-voice — and turns your words into text. If it doesn't catch you, run notchfolkd --doctor to check the microphone.

Where is my data stored?

On your computer: tasks, reminders, and files in ~/.local/share/notchfolk, settings in ~/.config/notchfolk. There are no accounts and no tracking.

Is there an AUR package?

Not yet. notchfolk-bin is prepared, but AUR registration is paused, so it isn't published. The install command above is the way to install for now.

Privacy

Notchfolk doesn't collect personal data and has no analytics. Your tasks, reminders, files, clipboard history, and settings are saved only on your computer, in ~/.local/share/notchfolk and ~/.config/notchfolk.

Prayer times are calculated on your computer from the city you choose. Voice is transcribed by local Whisper on your device, or by the speech router you configure yourself — nothing is sent anywhere you have not set up.

When you use synced lyrics, Notchfolk sends the song title, artist, and length to LRCLIB (lrclib.net) to find matching lyrics.

When you use an AI provider, your request goes to that provider, as you chose in Settings. The MCP server runs on your computer (localhost); if you expose it with a tunnel, only the people and tools you give the address to can reach it.
